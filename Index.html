5 Facility layout

By default Coordinate assumes any two fields at the same venue are close enough for one official to work back-to-back. If your site has fields far enough apart that this isn't realistic — a turf complex where field 1 and field 12 are a hike, diamonds where 1 and 30 need a car — mark those pairs here. This is a hard rule: it applies no matter what you pick for the back-to-back policy below, because it isn't a preference, it's geography.
